# Frozen decision contract — initial seven-indicator baseline

Date: 2026-07-30

The initial baseline contains seven contemporary indicators. Responsible AI is
deferred after its blind validation failure and is not part of this contract.

## Evidence families

1. **Frontier production**
   - notable AI models;
   - academia–industry composition, conditional on observing at least one model.
2. **Scientific presence**
   - equal-venue fractional share across the seven frozen venues/tracks.
3. **Infrastructure**
   - TOP500 systems;
   - aggregate TOP500 Rmax.
4. **Economic conversion**
   - private AI investment;
   - newly funded AI companies.

Indicators are not combined into a score. Results are reported as absolute
values first and normalized values only when the denominator is compatible.

## Decision rules

- `supports`: Brazil exceeds the relevant peer reference and the result is not
  dependent on a single source, venue, or normalization.
- `challenges`: Brazil is below the peer reference or absent from a frontier
  universe, with adequate source coverage.
- `mixed`: Brazil is regionally strong but materially distant from the global
  frontier, or direction changes across valid lenses.
- `inconclusive`: the observation is censored, undefined, or too incomplete to
  establish direction on its own.

Absence from Epoch's notable-model universe challenges observed frontier
presence; it does not prove absence of domestic capability. Absence from the
AI Index top 15 is a censored upper bound, never a zero.

## Baseline classification

`H-BASE-001` is supported only if Brazil shows above-peer results in multiple
evidence families and those results survive normalization and sensitivity
analysis. It is challenged if favorable evidence is confined to one family or
is regional while frontier/scientific/economic-conversion evidence remains
weak. Otherwise it is mixed or inconclusive.

The main hypothesis `H-MAIN-001` cannot be decided by this baseline alone.
Specific sector advantages require evidence of differentiated capability,
conversion into outcomes, and local value capture.
