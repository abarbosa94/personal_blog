# Draft validation contract — Responsible AI accepted papers, 2025

The study adapts the HAI indicator from submissions to accepted or published
papers because rejected submissions and their affiliations are not publicly
observable in a comparable way. The frozen venues are AAAI, AIES, FAccT, ICLR,
ICML, and NeurIPS.

The thematic dimensions follow the HAI codebook:

1. privacy and data governance;
2. transparency and explainability;
3. security and safety;
4. fairness.

Title matching is only a high-recall candidate screen. Explicit dimension
phrases create candidates. Ambiguous words—including generic bias, safety,
security, robustness, trustworthy, accountability, and ethics—require
contextual review and never create a final positive label alone.

Before country aggregation, a stratified sample must include explicit
candidates, ambiguous candidates, and title-screen negatives checked using
abstract/full-text context. Precision and estimated missed-positive rate must
be reported. The final classifier and acceptance threshold remain unfrozen
until this validation is complete.
