# Hypotheses for later sector case studies

These cases are investigative hypotheses, not conclusions derived from the
current baseline.

## Financial AI — Nubank and Itaú

Test whether Brazil's digital financial scale, transactional data, regulation,
and banking rivalry support locally developed AI capabilities and measurable
value capture. Evidence must distinguish proprietary/local development from
ordinary adoption of global models and cloud services. Candidate outcomes
include credit and fraud performance, service cost, productivity, adoption,
technical ownership, and external diffusion.

## Legal AI — Jusbrasil

Test whether Brazil's unusually large and digitized legal system creates a
defensible local advantage through domain data, language, workflows, and
distribution. Evidence must distinguish an advantage in legal information or
content access from an advantage in AI, and test productivity, quality,
technical ownership, paid adoption, scalability, and local value capture.

Jusbrasil states that its own legal foundation model was developed with
Maritaca AI and trained using its Brazilian legal database. This makes the
partnership a joint conversion case linking local model capability, proprietary
domain data, product distribution, and end-user adoption. The statement is
verified as a partnership lead, but performance and competitive advantage
remain untested.

## Brazilian Portuguese models — Maritaca

Test whether linguistic and cultural specialization enables a domestic model
producer to compete on Brazilian tasks despite limited frontier-scale capital
and compute. Comparisons must freeze Portuguese benchmarks and measure quality,
cost, latency, safety, adoption, technical provenance, dependence on foreign
base models/compute, and local ownership of revenue and intellectual property.
The Jusbrasil deployment is a priority downstream case for testing whether
Maritaca's linguistic specialization converts into domain performance and
commercial use rather than remaining a standalone benchmark result.

## Guardrails

- Case selection does not establish sector representativeness.
- Corporate claims are leads, not observations, until corroborated.
- AI use, AI-enabled products, and proprietary AI capability are separate.
- Domestic adoption, international competitiveness, and domestic value capture
  are separate outcomes.
- Contrary and null evidence must be collected for every case.
- Joint evidence from Jusbrasil–Maritaca must not be counted twice when
  synthesizing the sector and conversion hypotheses.
