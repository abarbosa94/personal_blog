# Frozen evidence for the initial sector cases

Accessed 2026-07-30. Corporate sources establish a mechanism or an observed
product, not a national competitive advantage. They cannot satisfy an outcome
gate without a defensible comparator and evidence of local value capture.

| Case | Evidence frozen for this post | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nubank | The 2025 results release attributes credit deployment to `nuFormer` and reports more than 10 million monthly active users for AI Pix. A June 2026 strategy note describes proprietary data and foundation models. | candidate |
| Itaú | The 2025 integrated report documents a generative platform using proprietary data and recommendation models; the investment agent began with 10,000 clients. | candidate |
| Jusbrasil | The product page reports a domain-specific assistant grounded in the company legal corpus and reports usage/satisfaction figures. | candidate |
| Maritaca + Jusbrasil | Company material and the product launch document the relationship between a Brazilian Portuguese-model producer and a legal application. | candidate |
